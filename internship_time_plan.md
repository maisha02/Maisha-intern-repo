# Internship Time Plan

## Overview

To stay consistent and complete my internship hours, I created a weekly plan based on my availability. I prefer focusing my work into fewer days so I can stay fully concentrated instead of spreading it across the whole week.

## Weekly Plan

### Main Work Days

- **Days:** Monday and Tuesday/Thursday  
- **Time:** 11:00 AM – 4:30 PM   
- **Location:** On mondays work from Swinburne, and the other day from Home 

### Total Weekly Hours

- Monday: 5.5 hours  
- Tuesday: 5.5 hours  
- **Total per week: 11 hours**

## How I Will Stay Consistent

- I will treat these two days like fixed work days and avoid scheduling other tasks during this time.  
- I will break work into smaller tasks (e.g., completing one issue at a time) to stay productive.  
- I will use Focus Bear to block distractions and maintain focus during sessions.  

## Time Logging with Focus Bear

I will use Focus Bear to track all my work sessions. Every time I start working on internship tasks, I will start a focus session.

This helps me:
- Track how many hours I actually worked  
- Stay focused without distractions  
- Build a consistent routine  

These logs will also act as proof that I am completing my required hours.

## Backup Plan

- If I miss a session on Monday or Tuesday, I will make up the hours on Wednesday or the weekend.  
- During busy weeks (like exams), I may adjust slightly but still aim to complete 11 hours.  

## Notes

This schedule works best for me because I can dedicate full attention on fewer days, instead of splitting my focus throughout the week.
