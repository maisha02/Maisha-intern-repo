# Competitive Landscape

## Focus Bear vs Other Productivity Apps

For this task, I compared Focus Bear with Freedom, Cold Turkey, Tiimo, and Routinery. While researching, I noticed that all of these apps focus on productivity, but they approach it in very different ways. Some focus only on blocking distractions, while others focus more on planning or routines.

## My Understanding of Each App

### Focus Bear
Focus Bear feels like an all-in-one system. It combines focus sessions, blocking distractions, and structured routines like morning and evening habits. From what I saw, it is designed for people who struggle not just with distractions, but also with staying consistent and following a routine.

### Freedom
Freedom seems very simple and focused. Its main job is blocking distracting apps and websites across multiple devices. It doesn’t try to manage your whole day, just helps you stay away from distractions.

### Cold Turkey
Cold Turkey feels stricter than Freedom. Once you block something, it’s very hard to bypass. It looks more suitable for people who need strong control and discipline, especially on desktop.

### Tiimo
Tiimo is very different from the others. It focuses on planning your day visually with schedules and timers. It seems helpful for people who need structure and clarity rather than strict blocking.

### Routinery
Routinery is focused on building habits and routines step by step. It guides you through tasks with timers and reminders. It feels more like a habit-building app than a focus or blocking tool.

## Reflection

### What makes Focus Bear different from these apps?
To me, Focus Bear stands out because it combines multiple things in one place. It’s not just blocking distractions like Freedom or Cold Turkey, and it’s not just planning like Tiimo or Routinery. It tries to handle the full flow of the day — starting routines, staying focused, and managing breaks. That makes it feel more complete compared to the others.

### If I were a user, why would I choose Focus Bear over competitors?
If I were choosing as a user, I would pick Focus Bear if I wanted everything in one app. Instead of using one app for blocking and another for routines, I can use Focus Bear for both. I think it would be especially useful for me when I feel distracted and also struggle to stay consistent with habits.

### What’s one feature that other apps have that Focus Bear doesn’t?
One thing I noticed is that Tiimo has strong calendar integration and visual scheduling. It lets users clearly see their day and plan tasks in a visual way. Focus Bear doesn’t seem to focus as much on that kind of visual planning.

### Based on your research, what’s one improvement Focus Bear could make?
Based on what I saw, Focus Bear could improve by adding better daily planning features, like calendar integration or a visual schedule. This would help users connect their routines and focus sessions to their actual daily tasks.

## Honest Conclusion

From my perspective, each app is strong in its own area. Freedom and Cold Turkey are better if someone only wants strict blocking. Tiimo is better for planning and visual structure. Routinery is better for simple habit building. Focus Bear is different because it tries to combine everything into one system. That makes it more flexible, but also means it may not be the absolute best in one specific area.
